# Images, volumes, and networks

Images are node-local inventory. Pull, inspect, remove unused images, and prune only after confirming no running or rollback workload depends on them. Repository-built artifacts should be tracked by immutable digest.

Gateway-managed local volumes are the supported destination for new mount changes. Deleting a volume is destructive and independent of deleting a workload. Back up stateful data before migration or recreation.

Networks provide workload connectivity. Compose-owned non-external networks are managed through the Compose Project. External or shared networks remain global and must not be removed while referenced.

When a node is offline, image/volume/network views may reflect last-known inventory. Wait for synchronization before cleanup.
