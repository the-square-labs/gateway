# Secure Links

Secure Links connect supported Gateway-managed resources without turning the destination into a general public endpoint.

## Database bindings

A managed database binding connects one standalone container, Deployment, or Compose service to one managed database. Gateway creates a separate engine identity for the binding and delivers only binding-specific connection material. The database owner credential is not exposed to the workload.

Recreating the workload keeps the binding available because authorization follows the stable Gateway resource identity. Deleting the binding retires its engine identity through a reconciled lifecycle; Gateway must not leave orphan roles or users.

## Ingress upstream links

Routes and Additional Routes can target supported Docker resources through a Route-owned Secure Link. Advanced configurations can use separately managed additional bindings. Route-owned bindings are visible for diagnosis but cannot be deleted independently from their owner.

## What Secure Links do not do

- They are not a general-purpose VPN or overlay network.
- They do not provide arbitrary TCP or SSH forwarding.
- They do not open host firewalls automatically.
- They do not remove the need for application-level authentication where the target protocol requires it.

## Operational checks

Monitor connector state, Relay availability, setup latency, active streams, throughput, completion health, and admission rejects. On failure, diagnose identity and desired-state reconciliation before recreating resources manually.
