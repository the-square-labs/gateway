# Plans and entitlements

Gateway uses Community, Personal, Business, and Enterprise plans. Availability, commercial-use terms, self-hosted licensing, and Wiolett Cloud quotas are separate concepts.

The authoritative current matrix is `docs/licensing.md`. Do not copy proposed pricing or old `homelab` plan terminology into customer material.

Ready paid features are enforced at the operation boundary as well as in the UI. Downgrade or expiry preserves resource data while blocking new premium operations and disabling switchable premium services according to their lifecycle rules. Grace periods and exact feature availability can change with product releases; review the current matrix before publication.

Community installations do not require a key. A Business or Enterprise self-hosted key represents one active Gateway instance; resource quotas inside a self-hosted instance must not be confused with separate Wiolett Cloud quotas.
